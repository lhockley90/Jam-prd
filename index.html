Help

When will my order arrive?

Each piece is printed to order, then shipped. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as it's on its way.

How do the tees fit?

Unisex fit. If you're between sizes, or you want it oversized, go one size up.

Can I return something?

You can return unworn items within 14 days of delivery. If anything arrives damaged or misprinted, get in touch and we'll replace it.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Yes. We ship worldwide, and shipping is worked out at checkout.
